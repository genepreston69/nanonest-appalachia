Complete communities

More than homes: everything a community needs

NanoNest communities are designed as complete ecosystems, with modular units configurable for sleeping, bathrooms, kitchens, laundry and more. Efficient to deploy and adaptable by design, they are built to support real lives from day one.

Sleeper pods

Private, lockable resident rooms. ICC-ES certified for occupancy.

For transitional, workforce or emergency housing where residents share kitchen and bath modules.

Large kitchen modules

Communal-scale kitchens with full appliances, prep areas and a dining bar.

A shared kitchen and dining hub that anchors a cluster of sleeper pods.

Shower modules

Multi-stall shower facilities. Water-system flexible.

Private stalls in one module, configured to the site’s water supply.

Restroom modules

Standard and ADA-compliant toilet facilities.

Paired toilet-and-sink stalls that serve a whole site from one module.

Accessible bathrooms

ADA-compliant accessible facilities, included in every deployment.

A row of wash stations plus a fully accessible restroom keeps every site ADA-ready.

Community modules

Fully customizable, designed to support a wide range of uses.

An open shell for a clinic, classroom, office, laundry or gathering space.

Laundry modules

Industrial-grade community laundry rooms.

Commercial washers and dryers with folding counters, so residents can do laundry on site.
